# How to use Steady

## Getting started
- Open https://turbulenceking5.github.io/steady/ on your phone and add it to your home screen. It shows up as "Steady" with a plain icon.
- Settings (⚙︎) lets you change your name, your boyfriend's name in the story, and the theme.

## Today
- A daily reminder, a mood check-in and a journal prompt. Writing or checking in keeps your streak going.
- Today's messages shows the next story chapter and a featured chat.

## Messages
- Everyday chats from friends, exes and the uni group chat, who all know you're gay and don't make a thing of it. Pick any reply; there are no wrong answers.

## Story
- From being in the closet with Archie, through coming out, to six months with your boyfriend. Pick a girl to date: each one's story goes date by date until she works out you're gay and goes off with a straight guy. Finish a chapter and the next one opens straight away.

## Style
- Dress up your avatar with clothes, shoes, accessories and makeup, and watch how the look reads.
- Save outfits and see how your friends react.
- The "wear it out" ladder: tick off each step in real life, from trying it on at home to wearing it somewhere busy.

## Practice
- Rehearse coming out and handling reactions. Write it in your own words (it helps) or pick a line.
- Your words keeps everything you've written.

## Progress
- Earn XP, level up, complete three daily quests, and unlock achievements.

## Privacy
- Everything stays on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Hide** covers the screen with a grocery list. Double-tap it to come back.
- Export a backup now and then (Settings), because clearing browser data erases everything.
