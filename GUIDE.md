# How to use Steady

## Getting started
- Open https://turbulenceking5.github.io/steady/ on your phone and add it to your home screen. It shows up as "Steady" with a plain icon.
- Settings (⚙︎) lets you change your name, your boyfriend's name in the story, and the theme.

## Story
- The app is the Story. It goes from being in the closet with Archie, through coming out, to six months with your boyfriend.
- Pick a girl to date. Each one's story goes date by date until she works out you're gay and goes off with a straight guy. Finish a chapter and the next one opens straight away.
- **Continue** at the top of the list opens the next unread chapter of the girl you last played. Each girl shows how many of her chapters you've read.
- Before most dates you pick what you're wearing. It changes how the date opens and both bars at the end: how gay she thinks you are, and how much she fancies you.
- After an in-person date, **In her head** shows what she was really thinking: her first look at your outfit, what she noticed and didn't say, and who she's telling.
- **Boy clothes:** on a girl's first date, pick "Boy clothes" and her story goes a different way. She keeps dating you if you keep it up, and moves on if you slip. Replay the first date in another outfit to get her usual story back.
- Messages type out one by one. Tap the conversation, or "Skip typing", to show the rest straight away. With Reduce Motion on (phone settings) they appear at once.
- **React** to any of her messages: tap it (or long-press it, or focus it with the keyboard and press Enter) and pick ❤️ 😂 😳 🙄 😬 or 👀. Tap the same one again to take it off. Your reactions stay on the chapter, so they're still there when you replay it.
- In text chats your newest message shows **Delivered**, then **Seen** when she's read it. Left on "Seen" with no reply means exactly that.
- **Quick replies** (Settings, off to start with): a timer runs over your reply buttons, longer when there's more to read. If it runs out, one gets picked for you ("Picked for you"). It pauses while Settings or Hide is open, or while you're picking a reaction.
- **Vibrate** (Settings, on to start with): a small buzz when you reply or react, on phones that support it (not iPhones). Off with Reduce Motion.
- Pick any reply; there are no wrong answers. "Try different replies" replays a chapter from the start, and **Change last reply** jumps straight back to your last choice (no retyping) so you can pick another.
- At the end of a chapter you can see how many of its endings you've found (and outfits you've tried). A girl's chapter list shows the endings count for each chapter you've read.
- Jess and your boyfriend's story open once you've finished any other girl's story.
- Your phone's back button steps back to the girl's chapters, then the list.

## Confirmations
- The **Confirmations** tab, next to Story, fills the screen with lines that fade in and out: "You are GAY", "Everyone knows you are gay" and more, some with your name. Just leave it open and read.

## Privacy
- Everything stays on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Hide** covers the screen with a grocery list. Double-tap it to come back.
- Export a backup now and then (Settings), because clearing browser data erases everything.
