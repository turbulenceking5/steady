# How to use Steady

## Getting started
- Open https://turbulenceking5.github.io/steady/ on your phone and add it to your home screen. It shows up as "Steady" with a plain icon.
- Settings (⚙︎) lets you change your name, your boyfriend's name in the story, and the theme.

## Story
- The app is the Story. It goes from being in the closet with Archie, through coming out, to six months with your boyfriend.
- Pick a girl to date. Each one's story goes date by date until she works out you're gay and goes off with a straight guy. Finish a chapter and the next one opens straight away.
- Jess and your boyfriend's story open once you've finished any other girl's story.
- Pick any reply; there are no wrong answers. "Try different replies" replays a chapter.

## Privacy
- Everything stays on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Hide** covers the screen with a grocery list. Double-tap it to come back.
- Export a backup now and then (Settings), because clearing browser data erases everything.
